GETTING STARTED

Use the calendar without an account.

Goat AI Calendar works without registration. Browse the month, compare Gregorian and lunar dates, and choose between your device’s local date and China Standard Time (UTC+8).

COMMON QUESTIONS

Why is my date different?

The app can use your local civil date or China Standard Time. Open Settings and switch the time basis to match how you want to read the calendar.

Does the app upload my birth details?

No. In the current version, optional birth details stay on your device and are not sent to a server.

How do I report a problem?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.
